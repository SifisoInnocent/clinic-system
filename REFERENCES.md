## Reference Format

All references follow APA 7th edition formatting guidelines. URLs were accessed in May 2025 and represent the most current versions of the cited resources at the time of CCAMS development.

## Implementation Notes

The CCAMS system implements concepts and best practices from the above references, with particular emphasis on:

1. **Django best practices** from official documentation
2. **Security principles** from OWASP and NIST guidelines
3. **Database design** following Chen's ER model principles
4. **User experience** based on Nielsen's usability heuristics
5. **Software engineering** practices from Sommerville and Pressman
6. **Healthcare IT standards** relevant to South African context

These references provide the theoretical foundation for all architectural decisions and implementation choices made during CCAMS development.
